# Pilot offer and outreach package (for founder review — nothing is sent automatically)

Rules: no invented results, testimonials or recovery rates. Every message is sent by the
founder, personally, after review.

## Recommended first segment (a hypothesis to test, not a finding)

**Training academies** (professional courses, tech bootcamps, exam preparation) over real estate,
for the first pilot:

| | Training academies | Real estate agencies |
|---|---|---|
| Sales cycle | Days to weeks (cohort start dates) | Weeks to months |
| Clear "won" event | Registration or payment | Sale or lease, often off-WhatsApp |
| Volume of enquiries | High | Lower, larger tickets |
| Attribution in a 4–6 week pilot | Feasible | Hard |
| Stalled-chat pattern | "How much?" → silence | Inspections, negotiation, multiple people |

Real estate stays a candidate for a second pilot once the measurement method has worked once.
Revisit this choice if the first conversations show academies have too few stalled leads.

## A. One-paragraph explanation
> Many enquiries on WhatsApp go quiet after a price or a follow-up. We review stalled
> conversations you choose to share, find where and why the conversation lost momentum, using only
> what was actually said, and give you one recommended follow-up message, checked by a person,
> that you can send yourself if you agree with it. You decide what to send. We never contact your
> customers.

## B. WhatsApp opening message
> Hello [Name], I'm [Founder], building a small service for businesses that get many WhatsApp
> enquiries that go quiet. I'm looking for 2–3 [training academies] to try it with me for a few
> weeks, supervised by me personally. Would a 15-minute chat this week be useful?

## C. Reply to an interested business
> Thank you. How it works: you choose a few stalled enquiries; I review each one and send you one
> suggested follow-up with the reasoning; you decide whether to send it; and you tell me what
> happened. For the first weeks we'll use example conversations to show you the output, before any
> of your customer data is involved. When is good for a quick call?

## D. Reply to a sceptical business
> That's fair. I won't promise a recovery rate, because I don't have one yet; that's what the pilot
> measures. What you'll see is a specific reason each conversation stalled and a message you can
> judge for yourself. If the suggestions aren't useful after a few cases, we stop, at no cost to you.

## E. Pilot invitation
> **Pilot: recovering stalled WhatsApp enquiries (4 weeks, 2–3 businesses)**
> - **You submit:** up to 10 stalled enquiries (text or screenshots), only ones you're allowed to share.
> - **You get:** for each, the likely sticking point and one founder-checked follow-up message.
> - **Private:** our internal analysis stays with us. You see only the approved message and status.
> - **You do:** decide, send it yourself, and report the outcome (reply, registration, no reply).
> - **Measured:** replies and registrations from recovered enquiries, against a simple baseline.
> - **Cost:** free for the pilot, in exchange for honest outcome reports and a pricing conversation at the end.
> - **Not guaranteed:** that any particular prospect replies or buys.
> - **Real customer data** is only accepted after we've agreed the data-handling terms.

## F. Five qualification questions
1. Roughly how many WhatsApp enquiries do you get a month, and how many go quiet?
2. What does one new customer or registration bring in? (A range is fine.)
3. Who replies to enquiries today, and do you follow up after silence?
4. Can you tell, later, whether a given enquiry registered or paid?
5. Are you able to share enquiry conversations with a service provider (consent and policy)?

Qualify if: 30+ enquiries a month, a meaningful value per sale, someone who can send the
follow-ups, and outcomes that can be tracked.

## G. Success-measurement template (per business, per case)

| Case ref | Date stalled | Value at stake (estimate) | Message approved (Y/N) | Sent by business (Y/N, date) | Prospect replied (Y/N, date) | Registered/paid (Y/N, amount) | Evidence of attribution | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Pilot totals: businesses invited → interested → agreed; cases submitted → usable evidence →
analysed → approved → attempted → replies → sales; revenue **with evidence** of attribution;
businesses willing to pay (and at what price).

**Attribution rule:** count a sale only if the prospect replied to the recovery message (or
clearly re-engaged after it) and then bought. Everything else is "unattributed".

## H. Founder demonstration script (10 minutes, synthetic data only)
1. (1 min) The problem: show a synthetic stalled chat ("How much?" → price → silence).
2. (2 min) Submit it as a business on a phone (Add prospect → paste conversation → submit).
3. (3 min) Founder view: open the case, run the analysis, show the evidence-grounded finding,
   the alternative explanations, and the reviewer's challenge. Explain that this view is private.
4. (2 min) Approve or edit the message. Switch to the business view: only the message appears.
5. (1 min) Business marks "I sent it", then records the outcome.
6. (1 min) Pilot terms and next step.

## I. Pricing validation
Test, don't assume. At the end of the pilot ask each business:
1. "If this continued, which would you prefer: (a) a monthly fee for up to N cases, or (b) a fee
   only when a recovered enquiry pays?"
2. For (a): "At what monthly price would this be an easy yes? Too expensive to consider?"
   Hypothesis to test: ₦30,000–₦75,000 a month for up to 20 cases.
3. For (b): "What share of a recovered sale would feel fair?" Hypothesis: 5–10%.
4. Strongest signal: **agreement to pay for the next month** (a payment link or invoice), not
   stated intent.
