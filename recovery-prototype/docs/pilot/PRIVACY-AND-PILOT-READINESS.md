# Privacy and pilot-readiness checklist (DRAFT — needs legal review)

Status: **draft for the founder and a Nigerian data-protection adviser.** Nothing here is legal
advice, and nothing here means compliance is complete. Until every item marked **REQUIRED BEFORE
REAL DATA** is done, demonstrations and pilots use **synthetic conversations only**. Businesses
may be recruited before then.

## 1. What data the product handles

| Data | Whose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Business account (name, email) | The business (our customer) | Supabase (staging project) |
| Conversation text or screenshots | The business **and its prospect** (a third party who never signed up) | Supabase storage; with paid analysis, sent to the AI provider |
| Analysis, review, recommendations | Generated by us | Supabase, founder-only |
| Outcome reports | The business | Supabase |

The sensitive part is the **prospect's** data. The prospect has no relationship with us, and may
not expect their WhatsApp messages to be read by a third party or processed abroad.

## 2. Legal frame to confirm with an adviser (Nigeria)

- **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)** and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission's
  implementing directive (GAID). Confirm the current version and what applies to us.
- **Roles.** Most likely the business is the **controller** of its prospects' data and we are its
  **processor**. That needs a written data-processing agreement. Confirm.
- **Lawful basis** for the business sharing prospect conversations with us (for example its
  legitimate interest in following up its own enquiries). Is a notice to prospects needed?
- **Cross-border transfer.** The AI provider (Moonshot AI, Kimi) is based in **China**, and
  Supabase hosting is outside Nigeria. NDPA transfer rules apply. Confirm the permitted basis, or
  choose an alternative provider or region for real data.
- **Registration.** Whether we, or a pilot business, must register with the NDPC (thresholds).
- **Sensitive data.** Conversations can contain health, financial or religious details. Decide on
  a rule: refuse such cases, or remove the details first.

## 3. Checklist

### REQUIRED BEFORE REAL DATA
- [ ] Adviser review of section 2. A written decision on roles, lawful basis and cross-border transfer.
- [ ] **Data-processing agreement** with each pilot business (draft: section 4).
- [ ] **Business authorisation**: the business confirms it may share these conversations with us for this purpose.
- [ ] **AI provider decision for real data.** Review Moonshot's data-retention and training terms in
      writing, or choose a provider whose terms and region are acceptable. Record the decision.
- [ ] **Data minimisation rule** for submissions: no ID numbers, bank details, health
      details or unrelated people. Prospect names are reduced to a first name or initial where
      possible. The business is told this before submitting.
- [ ] **Retention and deletion.** A fixed period, for example 90 days after the pilot ends, then
      deletion of conversations and screenshots. A deletion procedure exists and has been tested
      once on synthetic data.
- [ ] **Subprocessor list** given to each business: Supabase (database and storage), Cloudflare
      (web hosting), the AI provider.
- [ ] **Breach procedure.** Who to notify, and how fast (confirm the NDPA timeline).
- [ ] **Access**: only the founder account can read analyses. *(Technically enforced and tested — see section 5.)*

### ALREADY IN PLACE (technical)
- Businesses see only their own cases, and only founder-approved messages. Enforced in the
  database (row-level security), tested 51/51 on staging.
- The AI never contacts anyone. A person sends every message, manually.
- Paid AI calls are off by default. Each paid analysis is limited to USD 1 and is founder-started only.
- Provider keys live only in server-side function secrets, never in the browser or the code.

### BEFORE A COMMERCIAL LAUNCH (after the pilot)
- [ ] Privacy notice and terms of service published.
- [ ] Production project separate from staging.
- [ ] Self-service deletion request route for businesses.

## 4. Draft data-processing terms (summary for the adviser)

1. **Purpose:** analyse stalled sales conversations the business submits, and recommend a follow-up the business sends itself.
2. **Instructions:** we process only to provide the service, and never contact prospects.
3. **Confidentiality:** only the founder accesses submissions and analyses.
4. **Subprocessors:** as listed. Changes are notified.
5. **Security:** encrypted transport, access control enforced in the database, provider keys server-side only.
6. **Transfers:** as decided under section 2.
7. **Retention:** deleted N days after the pilot ends, or earlier on request.
8. **Assistance:** we help the business answer any prospect request about their data.
9. **Breach notice:** without undue delay.
10. **The business warrants** it may lawfully share the conversations it submits.
